Our Story

Pioneering Commercial Mobility in East Africa

Established under UTU Cars Africa, our commercial EV division was created to transform regional freight and logistics through zero-emission heavy transport solutions.

By combining established automotive manufacturing practices with cutting-edge electric mobility technology, we bridge global EV innovation with local assembly standards. We build and support commercial haulers customized for high payloads, tough terrains, and maximum cost efficiency across Kenya.

Our Mission

Built Local: Kenya Vehicle Manufacturers Assembly

Our mission is to support local technical capacity and build Kenya's electric commercial-vehicle ecosystem. Every truck is brought together through a structured local assembly process at Kenya Vehicle Manufacturers (KVM) in Thika, Kenya.

Starting from supplied Completely Knocked Down (CKD) components, our trucks undergo systematic mechanical, electrical, and drivetrain integration. This hands-on process builds deep local technical capability for long-term maintenance, inspection, and after-sales support.
