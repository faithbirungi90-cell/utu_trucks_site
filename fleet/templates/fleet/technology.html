Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding UTU electric truck technology, charging, and telemetry systems.

What battery technology powers UTU electric trucks?
UTU electric trucks utilize high-density CATL Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) batteries equipped with liquid thermal management systems for optimal temperature control and long lifespan across heavy usage cycles.
How does real-time telematics and CAN-Bus monitoring work?
Every vehicle integrates 4G-enabled CAN-Bus telematics that continuously relay battery health, motor temperature, speed, location, and predictive maintenance alerts directly to fleet managers.
What charging standards and speeds are supported?
Our commercial fleet vehicles support fast DC charging protocols (CCS Combo 2 / GB/T), allowing up to 80% charge restoration in under 45 minutes depending on the charging station capacity.
